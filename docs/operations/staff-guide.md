# ShopKhata staff guide

Use your own account. Do not share passwords or leave a signed-in device unattended.

- **Sign in:** open the owner-provided HTTPS address. Sign out when finished on a shared device.
- **Invoice:** choose the correct shop and business date, enter the source invoice details, review warnings and totals, then submit once. Confirm the result before moving to the next document.
- **Payment:** search for and open the existing invoice/customer record. Check the amount and due before recording. If the screen is slow or the connection drops, do not create another payment. Keep the form open, use its retry guidance, or ask the owner to confirm the result.
- **Customer due:** use the customer ledger and effective due shown by ShopKhata. Do not keep a private editable balance as a replacement for the ledger.
- **Expense:** enter the real expense once. Attach a supported JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF receipt (maximum 10 MiB). If upload fails, the expense record can still exist; tell the owner and retry attaching to that same expense.
- **Correction:** use the available correction/replacement flow and provide the required reason. Never invent a replacement invoice/payment or ask anyone to delete history.
- **Daily close:** only an authorized manager/owner closes a date. Review the business date and totals first. Escalate a mismatch before closing; reopen only through the authorized flow with a reason.
- **Historical search:** use invoice/customer search and verify shop and identifier before acting. Do not change business dates to make an old record appear in today's list.
- **Excel import:** use the supplied template, inspect every row and warning, and submit the batch once. Limit is 100 invoice rows. Never import the same batch again because the page feels slow.
- **Offline:** invoice drafts may be stored on this device. Offline does not save records to the server. Keep the same draft and retry after reconnect; do not create a second draft as a substitute. Receipts, payments and other server actions require a connection.
- **Unsure:** stop, preserve the current screen/reference, and contact the owner. Never manually edit production data, share credentials, or create a guessed balancing record.
